Research

Research Interests

Partial Differential Equations and Geometric Analysis, including nonlinear elliptic PDEs, geometric measure theory.

Recently, I was interested in following topics:

  • [December 2025 – present, second part of my thesis] Study of the De Giorgi–Nash–Moser theory (1960s) for nonlinear elliptic equations and its geometric counterpart concerning ε‑regularity in geometric measure theory. For fully nonlinear equations, we examine the Krylov–Safonov theory, further developed by Caffarelli (1987) and Caffarelli & Lihe Wang (1993). Subsequently, Savin extended the theory to small‑perturbation settings (2007), whose techniques enable the establishment of a crucial Harnack inequality and lead to an improved flatness theorem—a refinement that played a key role in resolving the De Giorgi conjecture for dimensions 4 ≤ n ≤ 8 under the necessary assumptions.
  • [November 2025 – present] Study of double-exponential growth for the 2D Euler equations, building on the framework of small‑scale creation introduced in Kiselev and Šverák (2014). Recently, Andrej Zlatóš (July 6, 2025) extended this approach to unbounded domains and proved the desired growth on the half‑plane using a novel idea.
  • [October 2025 – present] Investigation of nonlinear instability in fluid dynamics. This includes the linearization method in hydrodynamic stability theory developed by Yudovich, the bootstrap argument introduced by Strauss and Guo in the 1990s, and its application to the Navier–Stokes equations by Friedlander, Pavlović & Shvydkoy (2006). Recently, in collaboration with Prof. Daoguo Zhou, we established nonlinear stability and instability for Rayleigh–Bénard convection under various boundary conditions by demonstrating exponential growth.
  • [September 2025 – present, first part of my thesis] Study of qualitative properties of semilinear elliptic partial differential equations, including monotonicity, symmetry, Liouville‑type theorems for certain Schrödinger operators, and energy estimates. This work is motivated by Berestycki, Caffarelli & Nirenberg (1997) and Alberti, Ambrosio & Cabré (2001). The research focuses on two main directions. The first concerns monotonicity of solutions to Δu + f(u) = 0 on the half‑space with f merely locally Lipschitz. The case f(0) ≥ 0 was completely resolved by Berestycki–Caffarelli–Nirenberg, while the case f(0) < 0 in dimension n = 2 was proved by Farina & Sciunzi (2017). The problem remains open for f(0) < 0 and n > 2, as discussed in Phuong Le's recent paper.
